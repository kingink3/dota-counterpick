# Engine parameters and their sensitivity

Generated by `npx tsx scripts/params-report.ts` (ADR-039). Values and arguments live in
`src/params.ts`; this file shows how much the ranking depends on each of them.

Method: 120 seeded random drafts (5 enemies; half also with 2 allies and a role, half with your
hero locked and 1-2 items seen on each enemy): 60 with hero picks, 120 with counter items, 56 with an
item plan ("For this draft"). Each parameter is moved alone to
the low and the high end of its range; **top-5 kept** = share of the baseline top-5 still in the
top-5; **#1 changed** = drafts whose first pick changed. Sensitivity = the worst "kept" of the
six comparisons: low >= 95%, medium >= 85%, high below. *Not exercised* = no output changed at
either end; the parameter is real but these drafts never reach it - its argument stands alone.

Kinds: *game* - a fact; *design* - a cap / floor / normalisation; *derived* - computed from
other choices; *expert* - a judgement, to be calibrated first where sensitivity is high.

## Arguments

- **`ITEM_TOOL_SCALE` = 0.8** (expert, counter; range 0.6 – 1). An item's tool counts less than the same tool in a hero's kit: the item takes a slot and gold, is often bought for another reason, and its cooldown competes with the hero's own. 0.8 keeps item routes clearly secondary without hiding them; below ~0.6 an enemy's Orchid would barely change the read, above 1 an item would outweigh a kit built around the tool.
- **`LANE_WEIGHT` = 1.25** (expert, counter; range 1 – 1.5). Reasons against your lane opponents count 25% more: the first 10 minutes are spent against them, and a lost lane delays the core items every other reason assumes. Kept small because lanes swap and rotate; at 1.5+ the lane would dominate a five-hero draft.
- **`TARGET_FLOOR` = 0.5** (expert, counter; range 0.35 – 0.75). Hero picks and counter items: a counter reason is weighted by the target's position, 1 for their position 1 falling linearly to this value for their position 5 (0.875 / 0.75 / 0.625 between). Shutting down the hero the enemy plays around decides more fights than the same answer to a ward-buyer, but supports with one key spell still matter, so the floor stays well above zero. At 1 every target counts alike (the behaviour before ADR-059); below ~0.35 answers to supports would stop showing.
- **`STACK_DECAY` = 0.5** (expert, counter; range 0.35 – 0.75). Hero picks and counter items: against one enemy the strongest counter reason counts in full, the next x this, the third x this squared. Several generic edges onto the same target (pickoff -> no escape, pickoff -> low HP, burst -> low HP) describe one kill, not three; without the decay a hero with many generic tools outranks one with a single decisive answer. 0.5 bounds what one enemy can add at twice its best reason. At 1 reasons add up freely (before ADR-059).
- **`ROLE_FLOOR` = 0.6** (expert, counter; range 0.4 – 0.8). Hero picks for a chosen position: the score is scaled by how usual the hero is there - 1 on its most played position, falling linearly with (games there / games on the main position) to this value for a position it is practically never played on. Counter reasons assume the hero gets the farm and the lane of its usual role; Clockwerk as an offlaner (19% of his games against 47% as a 4) keeps most of his score, a hero picked into a role one game in twenty keeps 0.6. At 1 the position list of the KB alone decides (before ADR-060).
- **`ROLE_MIN_SHARE` = 0.02** (design, counter; range 0.01 – 0.04). A hero is not offered for a position it is played on in under 2% of its games in our bracket (data/benchmarks.json), even when the KB position list names it: Kunkka, Night Stalker and Lycan as position 1 are at 0%. Below the line are stale KB entries, not rare-but-real roles (those start at about 3%: Venomancer 3, Doom 2). Heroes without data are not filtered.
- **`RESIST_WEIGHT` = 1** (design, two-sided; range 0.7 – 1.2). Enduring an enemy's tool is weighted like answering it: both decide the same fight. 1.0 is the neutral choice that needs no extra justification; the per-enemy cap (RESIST_CAP) keeps it from stacking.
- **`RISK_WEIGHT` = 0.5** (expert, two-sided; range 0.3 – 0.8). A risk (the enemy's tool hits what you rely on) is a reason to play carefully, not a veto: players itemise and position around it. Half the weight of an answer keeps offense the backbone (ADR-020); at 1.0 every hero with a long reliance list sinks.
- **`BLUNT_WEIGHT` = 0.6** (expert, two-sided; range 0.4 – 0.8). Your tool blunted by an enemy resistance (magic damage into BKB / Pipe) loses part of its value, not all: the resistance has a cooldown or a cost. Slightly above RISK_WEIGHT because it removes a reason you were picked for, rather than adding a danger.
- **`RESIST_CAP` = 1** (design, two-sided; range 0.7 – 1.5). Max resistance bonus per enemy: one enemy cannot be 'resisted' more than one full answer is worth, so a long resist list does not inflate a hero.
- **`RISK_CAP` = 0.8** (design, two-sided; range 0.5 – 1). Max penalty per enemy, below RESIST_CAP so a single bad matchup cannot bury an otherwise good pick (net-negative picks are still dropped, ADR-022).
- **`TEAM_FULL_AT` = 3** (design, team; range 2 – 4). Team-gap bonuses reach full size once 3 allies are known: with 1-2 picks most gaps are simply not drafted yet, and with 3 the missing roles are real. Scale = min(1, allies / 3).
- **`DAMAGE_MIX_WEIGHT` = 0.3** (expert, team; range 0.15 – 0.5). Bonus for the other damage school when your team deals almost one school: a one-school team is countered by one item (Pipe / armour). Small - about a third of a single counter reason - because damage mix is one consideration among many.
- **`DAMAGE_MIX_MIN_SHARE` = 0.3** (expert, team; range 0.2 – 0.4). The bonus starts when the minority school is under 30% of the team's damage: above that one item no longer shuts the team down.
- **`SYNERGY_WEIGHT` = 0.6** (expert, synergy; range 0.3 – 1). A curated combo with an ally adds 0.6 - less than a typical counter reason (~1): the draft tool's job is answering the enemy; a combo should tip near-ties, not outrank a real counter.
- **`SELF_TOOL` = 0.5** (expert, items; range 0.4 – 0.7). The hero's own tool at weight >= 0.5 (a core part of the kit: Drow's Gust 0.6 silence) makes an item for the same tool largely redundant. Below 0.5 the kit's version is situational and the item still adds.
- **`SELF_TOOL_FLOOR` = 0.25** (design, items; range 0.1 – 0.4). A redundant item keeps at least 25% of its value (x max(0.25, 1 - own weight)): a second source of the tool still covers the kit's cooldown.
- **`MID_CARRY_ATTACK` = 0.6** (expert, items; range 0.5 – 0.7). A mid with attack_reliant >= 0.6 builds like a carry (Shadow Fiend, Templar), below like a caster: 0.6 is the tag weight the KB gives to heroes whose damage is mainly right-click.
- **`BUY_COMMON` = 0.05** (expert, items; range 0.03 – 0.1). The hero finishes the item in >= 5% of games: a normal option for it (fit 1).
- **`BUY_RARE` = 0.01** (expert, items; range 0.005 – 0.02). Under 1% of games the hero practically never builds it (fit BUY_FIT_NEVER); 1-5% is occasional (BUY_FIT_SOME).
- **`BUY_FIT_SOME` = 0.6** (expert, items; range 0.5 – 0.8). An occasional item (1-5%) keeps 60%: there are builds that use it, but the draft has to call for it clearly. Must stay above PLAN_BUY_FIT_MIN (0.5), or every occasional item drops out of the plan unless its score is >= 3 - the sensitivity report showed a third of the plan vanishing at 0.4.
- **`BUY_FIT_NEVER` = 0.25** (expert, items; range 0.1 – 0.4). An item the hero practically never builds keeps 25%: shown only when the draft calls for it strongly (score >= 3 survives the filter).
- **`PLAN_BUY_FIT_MIN` = 0.5** (design, items; range 0.3 – 0.6). An answer enters the plan when its buy fit is at least 0.5 - i.e. a usual (1) or occasional (BUY_FIT_SOME) item; one the hero never builds (BUY_FIT_NEVER) needs PLAN_STRONG_SCORE. It is the line between the two lower fit levels, not a tuned number.
- **`PLAN_STRONG_SCORE` = 2** (expert, items; range 1.4 – 2.7). An item the hero practically never builds still enters the plan at engine score >= 2: several solid reasons against their cores, i.e. most of the enemy lineup calls for it. Was 3 before ADR-060; item scores became smaller once reasons were weighted by the target and saturated per enemy, and 2 keeps the same share of item recommendations above the line (2% over 400 random drafts; the old range 2 - 4 maps to 1.4 - 2.7).
- **`PLAN_ITEM_FIT_MIN` = 0.6** (design, items; range 0.5 – 0.7). Items whose archetype fit (item_fit.yaml) is below 0.6 are not planned at all: in that table 0.6 marks 'possible for this archetype', lower values 'wrong archetype' (a support's Divine Rapier).
- **`ALLY_BUYS_SHARE` = 0.25** (expert, items; range 0.15 – 0.4). A teammate 'usually builds' an answer when they finish it in >= 25% of their games: then agree who buys, rather than both.
- **`ALLY_BUYS_SCALE` = 0.5** (expert, items; range 0.3 – 0.7). An answer a teammate usually builds is halved for you: still useful twice (two Force Staffs), but the second copy is worth less than the first.
- **`PIERCE_DISCOUNT` = 0.5** (expert, items; range 0.3 – 0.7). A defensive item that their lineup pierces (Black King Bar vs Doom, Chronosphere, Black Hole, Berserker's Call) loses up to this share of its score, reached when the piercing tools sum to 1.5 (about two heroes built around one). BKB still blocks the rest of their spells, so it is halved rather than removed; at 0 piercing disables are ignored (before ADR-059).
- **`KB_PP_PER_POINT` = 1.2** (derived, stat; range 0.8 – 1.6). Converts KB points into percentage points of win rate: the slope of the measured matchup gap on the KB lift (score against the enemy minus the hero's baseline) over 12 199 hero pairs with at least 1000 games, weighted by precision - 1.19 (npm run kb:validate, KB_VALIDATION.md). Noise in the gap does not bias a slope. Re-derive after a statistics refresh or a KB pass.
- **`STAT_PRIOR_GAMES` = 1600** (derived, stat; range 800 – 3200). Games at which a measured matchup weighs as much as the mechanics: edge = (games x gap + 1600 x prior) / (games + 1600). It is the sampling variance of a win rate per game (2500 pp^2) divided by the variance of the true gap around the KB prior (1.25 pp squared, estimated on pairs with 5000+ games after removing sampling noise) - the standard shrinkage weight, not a tuned number. At 1600 games the two count equally; at 16 000 the data is 91% of the estimate. Replaces the +-6% cap of ADR-035 (ADR-061).
- **`ROLE_DATA_SHARE` = 0.2** (design, counter; range 0.15 – 0.3). A hero is also offered for a position missing from its KB list when it is played there in at least a fifth of its games in our bracket (data/benchmarks.json): Broodmother 2, Chaos Knight 3, Wraith King 3, Gyrocopter 4. One game in five is a real role, not an experiment; the role-fit factor still lowers it against the hero's main position.
